# Knowledge Work in XR, an Overview & Experience

*This is a test post based on some old notes*

My experience has shown me that some ideas, some interactions, can only be really evaluated when they are done to a polished level, not as concept or rough implementation. The work with spatial knowledge comes under that category, where the idea of knowledge in space, where you can have a ‘wall’ full of a category, such as people’s names, and another with institutions seems reasonable and clear as an idea but how does that feel to use? How and when should the moves happen, what should spawn further options and how can the user see relationships?
